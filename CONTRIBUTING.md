## How to Contribute

### Reporting Issues
- Check [existing issues](../../issues) first
- Include: reproduction steps, expected vs actual behavior, relevant environment details
- Constructive disagreement is welcome; please focus on the content, not the author

### Suggesting Content Changes
- Small fixes (typos, broken links, factual corrections) → PR directly
- Larger restructuring or new sections → open an issue first to discuss

### Writing Style
- Keep technical accuracy first; mark reasoning vs cited facts (📎 / 🧠)
- Provide paper/code links for quantitative claims
- Chinese is the primary language; English welcome for direct quotes
